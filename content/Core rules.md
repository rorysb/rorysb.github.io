## Oh shit! You want me to roll? (Skills tests)

Rolls are based on rolling D20s.

The GM sets a difficulty (0-5) for which the player has to roll that many successes.

Successes are determined by a roll (per D20) equal or lower than the target.

### Critical success and catastrophic failure

A rolled 1 is a critical success = 2 successes.
A 20 is a catastrophic failure - bad things this way come.
### The target

The target is determined by adding the relevant attribute and skill.

### The dice pool

The dice pool, by default, is 2D20. This can be increased by purchasing further D20s with action points.

| Dice pool | Bonus D20s | AP cost |
| --------- | ---------- | ------- |
| 2d20      | -          | 0       |
| 3d20      | + 1d20     | 1       |
| 4d20      | + 2d20     | 3       |
| 5d20      | + 3d20     | 6       |
